# CONF-4 — Attempt timeline and callbacks

risk: high · surface: ui,security · lane: orders

Goal — Let confirmation agents review recorded call outcomes and schedule and find callbacks without leaving the confirmation queue.

## Done when

- The Confirmation sheet shows time, visible actor name and outcome for actual transitions to no answer or closed phone. Initial import statuses, delivery events and unchanged saves do not create attempts. Preserve the existing dial counter and its D10 write permissions. Label the counter and the outcome timeline separately. Historical counter-only dials have no actor, time or outcome to backfill.
- Admins and confirmation agents can schedule, reschedule or clear a callback on a visible actionable order before handoff. Save a single `timestamptz` instant from an explicitly labelled Africa/Algiers date and time. A callback-only save changes neither assignment nor status, note, attempts, stock or carrier state. Ineligible orders cannot retain a due callback that would reappear on reopening.
- Shared Confirmation seeds include Callbacks due and the Today confirmed seed deferred from CONF-1. Due includes non-null callbacks at or before the current instant, earliest first with a stable tie break. Today confirmed shows currently confirmed orders with a real confirmation transition today in Algiers, still under normal order visibility. Both views retain URL overrides, layouts, and Save as mine. Default queue and Shipping remain unchanged.
- CONF-4 supplies the schedule and due view. NOTIF-1 owns the due notification for the current assigned agent with existing notification preferences and visibility. Unassigned callbacks remain visible in the due queue without an invented recipient. Timing beyond on-load discovery stays with N-1 and NOTIF.
