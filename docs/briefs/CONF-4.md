# CONF-4 — Attempt timeline and callbacks

risk: high · surface: ui,security · lane: orders

Goal — Let confirmation agents review recorded call outcomes and schedule and find callbacks without leaving the confirmation queue.

## Done when

- The Confirmation sheet shows time, visible actor name and outcome for actual transitions to no answer or closed phone. Initial import statuses, delivery events and unchanged saves do not create attempts. Preserve the existing dial counter and its D10 write permissions. Label the counter and the outcome timeline separately. Historical counter-only dials have no actor, time or outcome to backfill.
- Admins and confirmation agents can schedule, reschedule or clear a callback on a visible order with confirmation status `wait_for_confirmation`, `no_answer` or `closed_phone`, delivery status `wait_for_deposit`, and no ZR shipment in its workspace. D10 queue visibility permits edits to another agent's callback on such an order. Save one `timestamptz` from an explicitly labelled Africa/Algiers date and time. The callback-only save preserves assignment, status, note, attempts, stock and carrier state. Leaving the actionable statuses or pre-deposit state clears the callback.
- Shared Confirmation seeds include Callbacks due and the Today confirmed seed deferred from CONF-1. Due repeats eligibility and includes non-null callbacks at or before the current instant, earliest first with order ID as a tie break. Today confirmed shows currently confirmed orders with a real confirmation transition today in Algiers under normal order visibility. Both views retain URL overrides, layouts, and Save as mine. Default queue and Shipping remain unchanged.
- CONF-4 supplies the schedule and due view. NOTIF-1 owns the due notification for the current assigned agent with existing notification preferences and visibility. Unassigned callbacks remain visible in the due queue without an invented recipient. Timing beyond on-load discovery stays with N-1 and NOTIF.
