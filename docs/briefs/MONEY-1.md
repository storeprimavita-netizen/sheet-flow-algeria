# MONEY-1 — Three-account ledger

risk: high · surface: security · lane: money

Provision ZR, DZD and USDT balances in every workspace, preserve legacy cash records, and record a ZR payout as one atomic transfer.

## Done when

- Every workspace has one system account of each kind. ZR and DZD use DZD. USDT uses USDT. Account identity, kind, currency and opening balances cannot be changed through the app.
- An admin can append a dated movement, record ZR → DZD and DZD ↔ USDT transfers, and reverse a movement or both transfer legs once. Balances count each movement once. Agents, anon and other workspaces have no financial access.
- D97 preflight shows the old balances, currencies, proposed destination and linked debits. A workspace with an unresolved row keeps its original accounts, balances and links usable. Posting the workspace is atomic and later remapping leaves audited reversals and replacements with original evidence.
- The Accounts and Overview reads keep working. MONEY-2 exposes mapping review, remapping and ledger controls. MONEY-3 moves paid-from recording to the ledger. Profit and KPI inputs are independent of ledger postings.
