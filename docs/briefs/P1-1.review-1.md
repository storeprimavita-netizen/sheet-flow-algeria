## Findings (ranked)

### M1 · medium · `supabase/migrations/00031_kpi_contract.sql:71-74, 88, 95`: `matured` ignores confirmed orders waiting for pickup
- **What:** for the delivery and return metrics, "unresolved" = `in_flight`, which counts only parcels after pickup. Confirmed orders still in `wait_for_deposit` count as resolved.
- **Failure (reproduced, rolled back):** 50 orders confirmed yesterday, 10 returned, 40 waiting for deposit. Result: `delivery {den:10, value:0, matured:true}` and `wilaya_stats.stop_shipping_auto = t`, while 80% of the cohort is unresolved.
- **Related:** the build reverses the "placed > 7 days ago" reading (recorded in D46), so old stuck parcels also count as resolved. That inflates the delivery rate, because returns come back slower than deliveries.
- **Fix:** unresolved = confirmed and not delivered / returned / cancelled-after-pickup, counting all of them or capping by age, as in the brief's wording. Align the D46 text.

### M2 · medium · `src/lib/pricing-real.ts:54-55`: Real scenario uses the wrong denominators
- **What:** `confirmation_pct` means "% of leads that confirm" (`pricing.ts:18`), but it now receives confirmed ÷ decided, which leaves out waiting orders. `lead_cost_eur` divides ad spend by all orders from the last 30 days, including excluded and duplicates, on UTC days. `delivery_pct` leaves out confirmed orders cancelled before pickup.
- **Failure:** confirmation is overstated, so cost per confirmed and per delivered is understated and the Real scenario's profit is inflated.
- **Fix:** use `of_received` for confirmation, or add a delivered ÷ confirmed key to the contract. Count `orders30` from `kpi_order_set`.

### M3 · medium · `src/app/app/dashboard/page.tsx:198-206, 242-243`: dashboard still filters by UTC day
- **What:** the today/yesterday strip and the CSV export use UTC or server-local dates and include excluded orders.
- **Failure:** an order at 23:30 UTC on 10 March shows in the 11 March tiles but in the 10 March export. The brief requires such an order to land on its Algiers day "on every screen".
- **Fix:** compute the bounds on the Algiers day, or take the export's ids from `kpi_order_set`.

### L4 · low · `00031_kpi_contract.sql` (`kpi_order_set`): `placed_at` can be NULL
- **What:** 2 seed orders have a NULL `placed_at`, and `webhook.ts:410,444` can insert one.
- **Failure:** those orders vanish from every dated KPI and count as "old" for `matured`.
- **Fix:** `coalesce(placed_at, created_at)`.

### L5 · low · `00031_kpi_contract.sql` (`pnl_by_product` → `kpi_summary`): cost grows with products × orders
- **What:** each product triggers a full order scan. `orders_kpi_idx` cannot serve the `(placed_at at time zone …)::date` predicate.
- **Fix:** an index on that expression, plus a set-based per-product query.

### L6 · low · `00031_kpi_contract.sql` (`product_stock_v`): `authenticated` keeps arwdDxtm
- **What:** 00030 revoked these rights on `product_stock_stages`, but this view keeps them. The view is not updatable (join), so there is no live exploit.
- **Fix:** revoke insert/update/delete/truncate, as 00030 did.

### L7 · low · agent rates and funnel consistency
- **What:**
  - A delivery agent sees only confirmed orders, so their `confirmation` is `{38/38, 1.0}`.
  - `stage_counts.received` leaves out swap replacements, while `per_product.orders_received`, `daily` and `shipped` include them, so the funnel can show delivered > confirmed.
  - `kpi_lines` accepts arbitrary `p_order_ids`, so a direct call skips the exclusion rules.
- **Fix:** hide or label agent confirmation, use one received definition across the funnel, and make `kpi_lines` intersect `p_order_ids` with `kpi_order_set`.
