## Findings (ranked)

### H1 · high · supabase/migrations/00031_kpi_contract.sql:101-108
- **Failure:** the code finds swapped originals only through a replacement that is in `p_order_ids`, then filters them again by the original's own `placed_at`. `swap_order` sets the replacement's `placed_at = now()`, which is always later than the original. So the original is counted only when both orders fall inside the range.
- **Reproduced:** on 2026-03-10, 9 delivered + 1 returned gives a delivery rate of 0.90. After the returned order is swapped, `kpi_summary('2026-03-10','2026-03-10')` gives 1.00 with den 9. All-time still gives 0.90.
- **Impact:** a refused parcel raises the rate, which D46/D47 forbid. This hits every closed period, every previous-period delta and the range-scoped `per_wilaya` stop-shipping flag. Review-2 M3 is back.
- **Fix:** select originals by their own attributes: workspace, their own Algiers day in range, product, eligibility, and in `dashboard_stats` the same filters. For example, `kpi_order_set` returns originals with a flag, or a sibling `kpi_swapped_set` is added. Never reach originals through the replacement.

### M1 · medium · supabase/migrations/00031_kpi_contract.sql:101-108
- **Failure:** the original's failure is credited to the replacement's wilaya, customer, agent and source, because `swap_order` accepts a new `p_wilaya_code`.
- **Reproduced:** original in wilaya 16, replacement in 31. `dashboard_stats(p_wilaya=>{16})` gives delivery 1.00, while `{31}` gives 0/1. `wilaya_stats.stop_shipping_auto` is wrong in the same way. A filter on `delivery_status=returned` drops the original completely.
- **Fix:** the H1 fix covers this. Originals must be filtered on their own columns.

### L1 · low · owner decision · D47 vs 00031 / kpi.test.ts:134
- **Mismatch:** D47 says the original "stays in the KPI rates as one received, confirmed order". The code keeps it out of received, confirmation and `of_received`, but adds it to the `delivered_of_confirmed` denominator.
- **Impact:** the Real scenario mixes two sets.
- **Fix:** the owner either clarifies D47 or the original is added to received/confirmed.

### L2 · low · owner decision · swap_order
- **Issue:** a swap is allowed from `delivered` or `exchange`. D47 then counts a delivered, paid parcel as returned, which deflates the delivery rate.
- **Fix:** the owner decides whether such swaps count as returned, or whether swaps from those statuses are blocked.

### L3 · low · 00031:195
- **Issue:** ROAS is not nulled under a wilaya, agent or status filter, so filtered revenue is divided by unfiltered ad spend. Under the same filters CPA and cost-per-delivered are nulled (D46).
- **Fix:** null ROAS under the same rule.

### L4 · low · kpi_rates grant
- **Issue:** `kpi_rates` is granted to authenticated and accepts any ids, so a direct call skips the exclusion rules. It returns rates only and stays inside the caller's workspace. Chained swaps (O→R1→R2) count only R1.
- **Fix:** revoke from authenticated (internal only), or intersect with the eligible set.

### L5 · low · data
