## Findings (ranked)

### HIGH
1. **Stock strip does not match spec band 5** — `src/components/dashboard/stock-strip.tsx:28-47`. STATS.md:78 requires "In stock · Reserved · On road · Low-stock items". The build renders a per-product list capped at 6 rows. It has no totals, no On road figure and no low-stock count, and Reserved is `hidden sm:inline`, so it disappears on mobile. Fix: add four summary tiles (total in stock, total reserved, on road, number of low-stock items linking to the filtered stock list) above the list, and show Reserved at every width.
2. **Scorecard has no "stock on hand" column** — `src/components/dashboard/product-scorecard.tsx:34-40`. STATS.md:80 lists stock on hand. `page.tsx` already fetches `product_stock_v`. Fix: pass `in_stock` into `ScorecardRow` and render the column.
3. **Scorecard is cut off at 390** — `product-scorecard.tsx:32`. The Net profit column clips ("Net pro", "-82 180 D") inside `overflow-x-auto` (en-light-390.jpeg). With no rate, "Set a USD rate" repeats on every row (en-light-390-norate.jpeg). Fix: use a card per product below `md`, or drop ROAS on mobile, and show the no-rate hint once, in the column header.

### MEDIUM
4. **Empty period renders false signals** (ar-dark-390-emptyperiod.jpeg):
   - The profit delta shows a green "↑ 0.0%".
   - The waterfall shows "−0 DA" on every line.
   - The scorecard says "No products yet." although products exist.
   - The stock band ignores the product filter.
   - An unknown product id shows its raw UUID in the filter chip.

   Fix: show a neutral or hidden delta when both sides are 0, show "0"/"—" with no sign, use a "no data for this period" empty message, and drop or validate an unknown product id.
5. **"To retry" count and its link disagree** — `src/components/dashboard/needs-action-band.tsx:29`. The count is no_answer + closed_phone. The link filters `confirmation_status=no_answer` only. Fix: make the link carry both statuses (or the saved filter the orders list supports). Other link gaps:
   - Cost per delivered (`money-band.tsx:76`) is not a link.
   - The profit tile goes to delivered orders, not to finance.

   The brief requires "each count/number links to its orders/finance list".
6. **Money band is lopsided at 1440.** Five tiles in a 3-column grid leave an empty slot, and the hero Net profit tile is about 70% empty (en-dark-1440.png). Fix: lay out 3+2 with the last tile spanning, or give the hero tile content (sparkline / previous period / margin) that fills it.
7. **Journey drop basis is misleading** — `src/components/dashboard/order-journey.tsx:36-37`. Delivered's drop (−47.4%, red) is measured against Confirmed, skipping On road, which is a different cohort (orders in transit now). The result is a red "leak" that is not one. Fix: label the basis ("of confirmed") and do not colour it as a drop, or make all stages one cohort.
8. **Filter bar placement** — `src/app/app/dashboard/page.tsx` grid, second child. A mostly empty full-width card sits between band 1 and band 2, and nothing on screen says band 1 ignores the filters. Fix: move the bar under the page header (or make it sticky) and mark band 1 "not filtered".
9. **Journey and waterfall heights are unbalanced at 1440.** The journey card is taller (two extra ZR rows), which leaves dead space under the waterfall. Fix: stretch the waterfall to the row height, or align the two cards with `items-stretch` and spread the content to fill.

### LOW
10. **Waterfall bars for small values** (return fees) are 2px wide, so they read as near-invisible at 1440 (`pnl-waterfall.tsx`). Fix: raise the minimum visible width and keep the value label next to each bar.
11. **Text and link gaps:**
    - The subtitle still says "Filtered overview…".
    - The "Set a USD rate" hint is plain text. It should link to the exchange-rate settings.
    - The retry link in `band.tsx` is a same-URL `Link` and may not re-run the server queries. Use `router.refresh()` instead.
