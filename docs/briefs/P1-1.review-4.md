### M1 — The drill-down list uses the UTC day, so it disagrees with the dashboard tiles
- **Where:** `src/app/app/orders/all/page.tsx:63-64`.
- **Failure:** the date filter is `gte("placed_at", from)` / `lte("placed_at", "${to}T23:59:59")`, in UTC. It also skips the excluded and unconfirmed-duplicate rules.
  - Every dashboard tile links to this page with the same from/to.
  - Example: an order placed at 2026-03-10T23:30Z is counted on the 11 March dashboard, but the 11 March drill-down does not show it; it shows under 10 March.
  - This breaks the brief's rule: "lands on its Algiers day on every screen".
- **Fix:** use the same `+01:00` day bounds as `dashboard/page.tsx`, and optionally an "eligible only" filter. Add a parity test at 23:30 UTC.

### M2 — `pnl_by_product` cost grows with products × orders
- **Where:** `supabase/migrations/00031_kpi_contract.sql:332-339`.
- **Failure:** the function calls `kpi_summary` once per product, with a cross join lateral.
  - Each call runs the `on_road` scan with a per-row EXISTS (147-155).
  - `pnl_lines` then rescans the whole workspace for the fixed-fee share (294-297).
  - Measured locally: 665 ms for 18 products and 1.1k orders, against 18 ms for one `kpi_summary`. That points to tens of seconds at 100 products and 10k orders.
  - The function runs on the dashboard, the finance overview and the products list.
- **Fix:** compute the workspace delivered count and the `on_road` totals once, or aggregate per product in one set-based query.
