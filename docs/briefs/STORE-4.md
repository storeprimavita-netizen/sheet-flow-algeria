# STORE-4 — Store checkout and thank-you page

risk: high · surface: ui,security · lane: store

Goal: accept live storefront orders into confirmation at the current configured price and give the buyer a private-by-design receipt.

## Done when

- A published product in a live store accepts one order per successful submission. Customer, order, selection and receipt save together. Retry returns the same receipt even if settings later change. The existing database assigns the order number, status history and duplicate flag. Stock stays untouched at placement, with later movements following D34 and D58.
- Charge the smaller of the valid quantity offer and the sum of current piece prices (D61), plus the selected positive DZD delivery fee (D14/D27/D60/D62). Mixed sizes remain valid. Ignore browser totals for computation. If the amount or selection shown to a buyer is stale, show the current choice and let them confirm it again before placing the order.
- Replay is safe under contention. Missing, draft, inactive or cross-workspace targets cannot order. Reject oversize bodies and invalid fields. Enforce bounded per-IP and per-normalized-phone limits, a honeypot and minimum fill time. Store no raw IP or phone in limiter rows or logs. No buyer data in URLs, public cache, error telemetry or another buyer's receipt.

## Pre-build choices to record

- One parcel/order must represent D61 mixed pieces and exact D14 totals. Current `orders` has one `variant_id`, one quantity and two-decimal per-unit `sale_price`. A total split over three pieces can be inexact under `sale_price × quantity`, and the current stock trigger moves one variant. Evaluate one parent with per-variant lines and an exact merchandise total, preserving legacy calculations for old rows. Confirm the representation and which legacy COD, KPI, confirmation, variant-edit and swap consumers need updates before building. Do not divide or round the buyer's charge or split a single parcel into multiple orders by assumption.
- The STORE spec expects distinct recent submissions to exercise the existing duplicate flag. The theme example says suppress the second same-phone/product order. Confirm whether a different request key creates a flagged candidate or suppresses it, and keep retry behavior independent of that choice. D28 applies to any change in what is charged, recognized as revenue, or visible to a role.
