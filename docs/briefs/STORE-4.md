# STORE-4 — Store checkout and thank-you page

risk: high · surface: ui,security · lane: store

Goal — Accept current, correctly priced storefront orders into confirmation and give the buyer a private receipt.

## Done when

- An accepted checkout creates one parent order and one parcel under D99. The order keeps exact merchandise and offer snapshots, the D14 delivery fee separately, and lines with quantities, quoted unit prices and selected variants. Customer, order, lines and receipt commit together. The order number, history and duplicate flag still come from existing triggers. A fresh matching request is eligible for the duplicate flag. A replay returns the original receipt even after settings change.
- Charge the smaller of the valid quantity offer and the sum of the selected pieces (D61), plus the eligible positive delivery fee (D27/D60/D62). The quote and submit use current authoritative configuration. An invalid or stale selection or amount requires explicit reconfirmation. Stock checks group repeated variants and use the shared D58 rule across the basket. Placement leaves stock unchanged. Later stock, permitted exchanges and swaps follow D34/D54.
- The application, exports, SQL revenue and carrier COD use the exact amount for line-backed orders and the existing unit price times quantity for older rows. KPI counts use one parent and costs use actual pieces. Existing role scope, recognition dates, exclusions and legacy order behavior stay intact.
- Only published active products in live stores can accept orders. Validate and bound input, enforce per-IP and per-normalized-phone limits, honeypot and minimum fill time. Limiter identities are digests. Receipt responses, cache, URLs and logs exclude buyer PII.
