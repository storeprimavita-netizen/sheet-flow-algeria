| D14 | Money model (owner, 2026-10-22). Customer pays **product price + delivery fee** (COD amount pushed to ZR = sale·qty + fee for the order's delivery type, `home` or `stopdesk`). **Income = product sale·qty only; the delivery fee is pass-through and never income or cost** (keeps the Phase-15 rule). Income is recognised when the order is **delivered** (`livre`) and shown as *money with ZR* until ZR pays it out; on receipt (`recouvert`/credited, D12) it becomes **cash**. `livre` is never counted as collected. **Net profit = income − product cost (COGS) − ad spend − other fees** (expenses, prorated fixed fees, return fee on returned orders). Delivery prices (owner CSV) are stored per wilaya × type to compute the COD amount to push and to check expected payout (amount − ZR deliveryPrice) against what ZR credits. | Assumes "product price" in the owner's profit formula means product *cost*. |
